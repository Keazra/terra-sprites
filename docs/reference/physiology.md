# `physiology.ron`

The body's fixed rules, which genes can't change (design §4.4, Appendix B): how fast a sprite burns energy and dries out, how it heals and dies, the ranges its genes are held to, and how strong the Cursor's touch is. Levels are fractions of full, from 0 to 1, and rates are per tick.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `newborn` | `(energy, hydration, stamina)`: what a new sprite starts with. Injury and the gut start empty. |
| `first_population` | `(low, high)`: the world's first sprites start with energy and hydration each drawn between these fractions of the newborn level. |
| `metabolism` | Energy used each tick: `basal`, plus `per_sense_tile` for each tile of sense radius, plus `per_step` for each step at speed 8 (scaling with the square of speed ÷ 8). Optional `resting`, not negative: what the basal and sense costs are multiplied by while a sprite rests (M2 design §19.2); 1 if left out, the same as standing awake. |
| `digestion` | The gut, and how what's in it moves into the body (M2 design §19.4): `(gut: 0.5, food: (pace: 0.001, waits: true), water: (pace: 0.002, waits: false))`. `gut`, above 0 and at most 1, since each thing in the gut stops at 1, is how much food and water it holds together: a bite or a drink that doesn't fit ends Too full, fires the `too_full` [pulse](loci.md), and takes nothing. Each tick, food moves into energy and water into hydration, each at most its `pace`; one that `waits` moves only as the body has room for it, and one that doesn't passes through, the body keeping what it can. Leave out `gut` for two guts with no limit, as packs from before Slice 29 have, and give a bare pace, `food: 0.001`, for one that passes through, so the old form `(food: 0.001, water: 0.002)` still loads. |
| `hydration_loss` | Hydration lost each tick. |
| `stamina` | `(per_step, idle, resting)`: stamina used by a step, and regained standing and resting. |
| `healing` | Injury healed each tick. |
| `injury` | `(starvation, dehydration, old_age)`: injury each tick while energy is at 0, hydration is at 0, or an old sprite's body has given out. A sprite dies at full injury. |
| `ageing` | Growing up and growing old (M2 design §9): `young_speed`, the share of its speed a newborn walks at; `growth`, the energy growing up takes, spread over childhood; `upkeep`, energy a tick for every 10,000 ticks of `grows_old`, for a pack that wants a later old age to cost something (0 in the built-in pack, M2 design §12.5); `wear: (low, starving, parched, injured)`, the lasting wear a tick while energy or hydration is under `low`, and for each unit of injury; `wear_speedup` and `sooner`, how much faster the most worn sprite's genes decay and how much sooner, as a share of `grows_old`, it grows old; `decay: (ticks, hit, gives_out)`, the ticks an unworn old sprite's genes take to fall to `gives_out` on average, `hit` health at a time; `scar: (above, chance, hit)`, the chance each tick that injury above `above` takes `hit` off a random gene; and optional `thirst_childhood`, above 0, which paces a young sprite's thirst to its childhood (M2 design §19.5): its hydration loss, and once it has dried out its dehydration injury, are multiplied by this over the ticks its genes take to grow up, so one that grows up sooner needs as much water. Left out, the young dry out at the grown rate. Optional: a pack without `ageing` gets the built-in one but without `thirst_childhood`, as packs from before Slice 29 have, so its young dry out at the grown rate too. |
| `breeding` | Having young (M2 design §10): `cost`, the energy an egg costs, split between a pair or all one's alone; `fed`, the energy and hydration a sprite needs above to be fertile; `rest`, the ticks after its last egg before it's fertile again, at least 1; `turns_female`, the `feminiser` level at which an adult male turns female for good; `alone_after`, how many ticks a female must go with no male adult seen or heard before her body lets her breed alone (M2 design §15.7); `receptive`, the `receptivity` level a female must have for a pair to mate (§15.3); `apart`, how many ticks two partners go with neither seeing nor hearing the other before they're freed (§15.4), at least 1; `drive`, the need that drives breeding, one of [`brain_io.ron`](brain-io.md)'s `needs`; and `crossover`, from 0 to 1, the chance at each gene that a pair's egg switches from taking its genes from one parent to the other (M2 design §12.2), 0.5 if left out, which is gene by gene. Whatever the genes, and whether or not the pack breeds, the engine has the drive work only between adults: it reads 0 in a sprite not grown up and in a choice about one, and its fall teaches worth only after a Mate, about that partner (M2 design §5). Optional: a pack without it gets the built-in one. A pack from before courtship says `breeds_alone`, the `longing` level a female had to reach to breed alone, in place of `alone_after`: it still loads, and keeps that rule. A pack with neither lets a fertile female breed alone whenever she tries. A pack without the `feminiser` and `longing` chemicals or the `fertile`, `female`, `female_near`, `male_near` and `bred` [loci](loci.md) has no breeding; one with them must name a `drive` that's a need, and needs an object type that hatches, the egg ([objects](objects.md)), for its sprites to lay. A pack from before eggs were items says `hatches`, the ticks an egg took to hatch: it still loads, its `egg` pseudo type turned into an egg item that hatches after that. In a pack whose egg is an item, `hatches` is refused: the egg's stage says when it hatches. |
| `calls` | Calls (M2 design §15.1), as `(cost: 0.002, reach: 300, heard_for: 150, listen: 10)`: `cost`, the energy a call costs the caller; `reach`, the most a call's way to a hearer may cost, by each terrain's `sound` in [`terrain.ron`](terrain.md), for it to be heard, so about 30 tiles over grass; `heard_for`, how many ticks a hearer goes on knowing who called and where from, at least 1; and `listen`, how many ticks a caller stands listening after it calls, at least 1. Calls and courtship work only in a pack that breeds and has the `receptivity` chemical and the `heard`, `courted`, `danced`, `lost_partner`, `partner_near`, `paired` and `condition` [loci](loci.md). Optional: a pack without it gets the built-in one. |
| `cause_fade` | Each cause of injury is remembered, halving every this many ticks, so a death names what did most of the harm lately (design §4.10). |
| `traits` | `(min, max)` for `speed`, `sense_radius`, `grows_old` and `grows_up`: what a `Trait` gene is held within. A pack from before M2 may call `grows_old` `lifespan`, and leave out `grows_up`. |
| `brain` | For every brain parameter, `(range: (min, max), default: d)`: what a `BrainParam` gene is held within, and the value a genome without one gets. Every parameter must be listed, and only those, except `place_fade` and `plasticity`, which packs from before M2 lack, `taught_rate` and `taught_fade`, which packs from before #187 lack, `befriend_rate`, `befriend_play` and `befriend_fade`, which packs from before courtship lack, `watch_rate`, which packs from before learning by watching lack, and `act_threshold`, which packs from before Slice 29 lack: they get the built-in ones. The `befriend_` ones are how fast a sprite in sight grows familiar each tick, the extra a Play with it adds, and how fast familiarity fades (M2 design §15.2). `watch_rate` is the share of the way a sprite's view moves towards what a sprite it watches knows (§18.1). `act_threshold` is how strong a pull it takes to act: a sprite with nothing to do starts only a verb scoring at least this, and with none stands where it is (§19.2); its default, 0, has it always start one. |
| `receptor_targets` | `(min, max)` for every receptor target in [`loci.ron`](loci.md). |
| `nearby_sprites` | `(radius, full)`: the `nearby_sprites` sensor counts other sprites within `radius` tiles, reading 1 at `full` of them. |
| `spawn_variation` | Each gene value of a new sprite is multiplied by a random factor between 1 − this and 1 + this, so no two are quite alike. |
| `trait_variation` | Optional. Traits that vary by their own share instead, as `{ "grows_old": 0.4 }`: each from 0 to below 1, named as in `traits`. A pack without it varies every trait by `spawn_variation`. |
| `mutation` | Optional. Copying errors when an egg is laid (M2 design §12), as `(chance: 0.01, small: 0.2, large: (share: 0.75, most: 2.0))`: each gene value has `chance` of changing, by a factor between 1 − `small` and 1 + `small`, or for a `large.share` of the changes, a factor from 1 ÷ `large.most` to `large.most`. `chance` and `share` are from 0 to 1, `small` from 0 to below 1, and `most` at least 1. A pack without it has no copying errors: its eggs' gene values are their parents' exactly. |
| `flaws` | Optional. Hidden flaws (M2 design §12.3), as `(arise: 0.004, founders: 0.035, health: 0.5, wear: 0.05)`. Each gene that can weaken has two copies, one from each parent, either of which may be flawed, and one sound copy is enough. As an egg's genes are copied, each sound copy gets a flaw with the chance `arise`; a sprite made from the starter genome has a flaw in one copy of each such gene with the chance `founders`. A gene with both copies flawed is broken: it's born with the health `health`, and counts as `wear` of wear towards when the sprite grows old and how fast its genes decay. `arise`, `founders` and `health` are from 0 to 1, and `wear` at least 0. A pack without it has no flaws. |
| `copy_and_change` | Optional. Whole genes doubled, lost or changed in what they do when an egg is laid (M2 design §13), as `(doubled: 0.00029, lost: 0.00029, changed: 0.00029, on_copy: 0.75, longest: 2.0)`. One roll for each gene decides which, if any, happens to it, with the chances `doubled`, `lost` and `changed`, which add up to at most 1. A doubled gene's copy goes right after it. A changed gene gets a new input it reacts to, action, thing it attends to, or life stages it works in, on a fresh copy next to the original with the chance `on_copy`, or else in its place. A genome never grows past `longest` times the starter genome's length, at least 1. A pack without it never doubles, loses or changes a gene. |
| `lesson_threshold` | How far from 0 a learned value must get before the event log announces the lesson. |
| `relief_deadband`, `touch_window` | The smallest fall in a need that counts as relief, and how many ticks after a try the thing tried is still what a feeling is about. |
| `forget_below` | A remembered sprite is forgotten once everything learned about it is nearer 0 than this, and a remembered place once it has faded below this. |
| `remembered_places` | `(held, per_kind, merge)`: a sprite remembers at most `held` places (M2 design §7) and at most `per_kind` of one kind, and places of one kind `merge` tiles apart or fewer are one place, so a pond is one place. `held` and `per_kind` must be at least 1. Optional: a pack without it gets `(held: 8, per_kind: 3, merge: 5)`. |
| `actions` | `(rest_bout, retreat_bout, timeout)`: a rest's length in ticks, a retreat's in steps, and how many ticks before any action gives up. |
| `movement` | `(flood_refresh, occupied_penalty, replan_after)`: how often a sprite re-plans its way, how much it avoids tiles with sprites on, and how long it waits when blocked before going round. |
| `cursor` | The Cursor's touch: the reward a `pet` and a `hug` give; the punishment and pain a `zap` and a `shock` give; `max_reach_back`, how many ticks back a pet looks for what the sprite just tried; and `furthest`, how far it throws or shoves a `small`, `medium` or `large` thing. |

Every number is checked when the pack loads: fractions from 0 to 1, rates not negative, each range's `min` no more than its `max`, and each default within its range.

## Working example

The built-in `data/physiology.ron` is the working example: it lists every field, with a comment on each. To make sprites thirstier, raise `hydration_loss`; to make a pet teach faster, raise `cursor.pet`.
