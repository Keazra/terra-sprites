## The play-through checklist

Play the build in Windows Terminal, at least 100×30 (140×40 is better), and tick each line that behaves as it says. It's ranked, as #226 settled: the four critical pieces in full, a line or two of each medium piece, and only the M1 lines that M2 changed. Play a Story world from New world as it comes, then fast-forward one Lab world and open it (G6). A note holds M2 up only if you call it critical; every note becomes an issue, as for M1.

### Starting up (M1's lines that M2 changed)

- [ ] `cargo run --release` opens on the title screen: in the dark, the Cursor's light finds a sleeping sprite, a world fills in around it, the sprite wakes, and the menu appears over the world, which keeps moving. Any key skips the scene.
- [ ] The menu's Continue opens your newest save, New world asks for a seed, a preset and a mode, Load lists the saves, Help and Quit do what they say. `--seed 7` goes straight into a world.
- [ ] In a world, `Esc` from Select asks to quit: `y` quits, `t` goes back to the title screen, and both save first; any other key keeps playing.
- [ ] `+` stops at 4× in a Story world ("4x is Story mode's fastest"); a Lab world (`→` on New world's mode, or `--lab`) goes on to 16× and Max, and the top bar says `lab`.
- [ ] `Ctrl+C`, in a world and on the title screen, quits at once and leaves the terminal as it was. `cargo run --release -- --force-panic` crashes on purpose and leaves it usable, with `last_session.replay` in the game's folder.

### A hard childhood (Slice 29, critical)

- [ ] The map is mostly dry, with a few big lakes, and the first sprites start near one.
- [ ] Sprites stand still more than in M1, and a standing sprite's doing line says `Watching …` or `Standing`. Grown sprites still get up and go when they're hungry, thirsty or bored.
- [ ] Newborns wander little. Left alone far from water, most die of thirst.
- [ ] **Save a newborn by hand.** Pick a young one with no parent in sight, and keep it alive until it grows up with only the Cursor: lead or carry it to water, put berries by it, pet it when it eats or drinks. It passes if you managed it and your help felt like what made the difference, rather than a chore.
- [ ] A sprite that tries to eat or drink again right after a meal says `Too full to eat …` or `Too full to drink`, and its Chem tab shows fullness rising, then fading.
- [ ] `--preset data/presets/gentle.ron` gives the old watery world.
- [ ] Nothing seems unfair or broken: grown sprites rarely die of hunger or thirst.

### Courtship, partners and families (Slice 32, critical)

- [ ] Within the first minutes, `♪` notes over sprites calling. Then the first dances, `♫`, and the log's first `… are now partners`.
- [ ] A sprite walking to a call says `Going to Bo #7's call` (with another name), and `v` adds `· by ear`.
- [ ] A selected sprite's Body tab has its partner, and its Brain tab the sprites it knows.
- [ ] Both partners keep near their egg, and if one goes off to eat or drink, the other mostly stays. After hatching, the young keep near a parent until they grow up, then go their own way.
- [ ] A sprite drawn light green is anxious: its egg, young or partner is out of sight. Its Chem tab shows `anxiety`, and it calls and heads back.
- [ ] Pick up an egg with the Cursor and put it down out of its parents' sight: a parent goes back to where it was (`Going back to its egg`), finds it gone, and gives up on the place.

### Breeding, eggs and hatching (Slice 23 and eggs as items, critical)

- [ ] Eggs (`o`) appear beside sprites, and the log says who laid them and, later, who hatched.
- [ ] A lone male grown sprite turns female for good after a while without seeing one (`… turned female`).
- [ ] A selected sprite's Body tab shows its sex, `fertile` while it is, and for a hatchling its parents. Clicking an egg says when it hatches and whose it is.
- [ ] In Grab mode, an egg can be picked up and put down somewhere else; a right click while holding it says `Can't throw an egg`. Held past its time, it hatches once it's put down.
- [ ] Only grown-ups court or mate. You'll never see a young sprite (`ö`) courted.

### Growing up and growing old (Slice 22, critical)

- [ ] A young sprite is drawn small (`ö`, or `a` with `--ascii`), walks slower, and grows up about an hour into its life at 1× (15 minutes at 4×); the log says so.
- [ ] A few hours in, the log says a sprite grew old. Its Body tab says `old`, and in time `wear` gains `· genes …` as its genes weaken; the Genome tab marks the weakened ones. It slows and dulls, and dies of old age about an hour later.
- [ ] The sprite list (`l`) has a life stage column.

### The medium pieces, a line or two each

- [ ] **Learning by watching (Slice 28).** Within the first hour at 1×, the log shows `… learned from watching …: water is good for thirst`, and a young one's eye moves to a parent heading for water or food it knows.
- [ ] **Memory of places (Slice 20).** A thirsty sprite with no water in sight says `Going to drink at the water it remembers · N tiles to go`, and its Brain tab lists its PLACES.
- [ ] **Children differ (Slice 24).** A hatchling's Genome tab opens with where its genes came from (`from … and …`) and what copying changed, and now and then the log ends a hatching with `with something new: …`.
- [ ] **The family tree (Slice 25, G2).** `k` on a sprite with young: its grandparents, parents, partner and young, the dead in dark gray with how they died. The arrows and `Enter` walk the tree; `b` until the status line says lineage colours its relatives on the map.
- [ ] **The population graph (Slice 26, G3).** `p`: the population over the whole run, split into eggs, young, adults and old. `←` `→` or the mouse read a moment, with its generations.
- [ ] **Fast-forward and Lab mode (Slice 27, G6).** After quitting, `cargo run --release -- --fast-forward 20 --seed 7` runs 20 hours of play with no screen, then `cargo run --release -- --load seed-7-20h` opens it: a Lab world at tick 90,000, whose `p` graph covers all 20 hours.
- [ ] **Room nearby (Slice 29b).** In that fast-forwarded world, near 100 sprites and eggs, the World tab says `world full` under `fertile 0`. In a world with room, a grown sprite with 4 or more grown sprites within 10 tiles says `too crowded to breed` on its Body tab; lead it well away, and once it's fed and rested it says `fertile`.
- [ ] **The title screen (Slice 21).** The first thing a new player sees reads well and plays smoothly (above).
- [ ] **The view past the wall (Slice 31).** Scroll past the map's edge: the glass runs round it, stars drift by in space, and the Cursor can't reach past the glass.
- [ ] **Also new:** `F3` shows the debug overlay in the map's corner, and `/` in the sprite list filters it as you type (a name, `young`, `gen 3`, `drink`).
