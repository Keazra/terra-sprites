## How it was measured

The simulation is the one on `main` since Slice 29b merged ([PR #350](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/350), `0294f39`); nothing that changes how sprites live has merged since, and this slice adds only the race's second world (`World::new_like`), which a world made with `World::new` never takes. As #226 (Q8) settled, a run counts where nothing that changes how sprites live has merged after it, so Slice 29b's family bars' run on the owner's PC, at `40dbe0a`, counts here.

## M2's criteria

| # | Criterion | Shown by | Where it stands |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Generations:** left alone, the population keeps going for several generations, neither dying out nor growing past the world's limit | The long run: a seed lasts when at the end it still has sprites or eggs, it never had fewer than 10 sprites at any count of its population record, and its sprites over its last 50 hours averaged at least half of hours 50–100. At least 9 of 10 must last (#222). The world's limit is the self-check's, every tick | ⟨PENDING⟩ |
| G2 | **Family:** any sprite's parents, grandparents and children can be seen, living or dead | The family tree's tests ([`family_tree.rs`](../../crates/terra-tui/tests/family_tree.rs), [`lineage.rs`](../../crates/terra-sim/tests/lineage.rs)), and the play-through | Met in Slice 25 ([PR #209](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/209)); the play-through looks again |
| G3 | **Population:** a graph shows the population rising and falling over time | The population graph's tests ([`population.rs`](../../crates/terra-tui/tests/population.rs) in both crates), and the play-through | Met in Slice 26 ([PR #201](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/201), with generations in [PR #215](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/215)); the play-through looks again |
| G4 | **Evolution:** after enough generations, the species has measurably changed from the starter genome | The long run, over the seeds that last: at least 3 gene numbers moved a tenth the same way in every one. If not, the race: the evolved species' young grow up more often than the starter's in the same fresh worlds, in at least 8 seeds (M2 design §19.6) | ⟨PENDING⟩ |
| G5 | **A harsh childhood:** 10% to 30% of unattended newborns grow up, in the middle seed of ten; at least 60% of the young hatched among sprites do; adults dying of hunger or thirst are under 1 in 20 of grown-up deaths | The unattended run and the viability run; the grown band in the long run too, over the run and its last 50 hours, in every seed that lasts (#226 Q6) | ⟨PENDING⟩ |
| G6 | **Fast-forward:** a world can run for many generations with no screen, and be opened afterwards | [`fast_forward.rs`](../../crates/terra-tui/tests/fast_forward.rs) (a fast-forwarded world is the one the game would have, and opens from Continue in Lab mode with its whole graph) and [`modes.rs`](../../crates/terra-sim/tests/modes.rs), and the play-through's fast-forwarded world | Met in Slice 27 ([PR #207](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/207)); the play-through opens one |
| G7 | **Content safety:** a child is never a mating target, for any genome or data pack | The self-check every tick, which every test and the soak run with; property tests with random genomes (`whatever_the_genome_only_adults_ever_breed_and_only_with_adults` in [`breeding.rs`](../../crates/terra-sim/tests/breeding.rs)); and the tests that no young sprite is courted, called or followed for mating ([`breeding.rs`](../../crates/terra-sim/tests/breeding.rs), [`courtship.rs`](../../crates/terra-sim/tests/courtship.rs), [`watching.rs`](../../crates/terra-sim/tests/watching.rs)) | ⟨PENDING: soak⟩ |
