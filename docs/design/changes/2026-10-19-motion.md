# Motion

Small effects drawn over the finished screen in real time, with one switch that turns them all off. Fauxpaw settled it in the UI lessons round of 2026-10-19 (question 7), and the six further ideas the same day, as Look 5 ([#306](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/306)) of the Look slices ([#301](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/301)), and it was built in [#393](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/393).

## M1 "A Sprite Lives"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Motion is drawn over the finished screen by tachyonfx,** in `terra-tui` only, timed by real time, not ticks. Each frame hands the app the time since the last, and tests and the renderer hand it a fixed step. Drawing stays pure: each frame builds every effect afresh from when it began. | #306 | §6.6, §6.8, §6.9 |
| 2 | **One switch turns all motion off,** on by default; `--no-motion` starts the game with it off. The older blinking, which tells the player something, stays. | #306 | §6.7, §6.9 |
| 3 | **A death flashes its tile and leaves a ghost** of the sprite fading to ash grey, since the sprite has left the world by the time the screen hears it died. **An egg about to hatch cracks,** its tile coming to glow and holding so until the young appears, since the screen can tell from the egg's stages that it hatches on the next tick. **A hatching sparks** round the egg's tile, and the young warms from the egg's colour to its own as the glow fades. Read for Fauxpaw on 2026-10-27 as the crack playing as the young appears, on the grounds that the screen can't tell in time when an egg will hatch; it can (`EggView::hatches`), so the crack is built before, as #306 asks. | #306 | §6.9 |
| 4 | **A tab opened sweeps in, a pop-up fades in and out, a drive's bar eases a block at a time, the view pans to a sprite or an egg, and a fading trail follows the selected sprite.** The call ripple waits for Slice 34's waves (#273). Water drifted between two shades in waves at first, but Fauxpaw found it odd in play on 2026-10-27, on a clock of its own and not joined to the shallow water, so it came out: water stays still in its theme's colours, and water that moves like real water waits for #399. | #306, #399 | §6.9 |
| 5 | **Few things move at once, and motion thins out at 4× and up:** deaths, hatchings and the trail are shorter and fewer, no egg cracks, and the bars and the view jump. | #306 | §6.9 |
| 6 | **The pan moves only what's drawn:** the viewport jumps at once, the pointer and clicks follow the drawing, and the Cursor goes to the tile under a still pointer only when the pan ends, so the world never hears of the tiles between. | #306 | §6.9 |
| 7 | **A theme's `motion` section gives motion's colours,** exact ones, and a named colour blends from how Windows Terminal's Campbell scheme draws it. A theme without the section uses the default's. | #306, #302 | §6.2, §6.9 |
| 8 | **Motion that tells the player something asks the `InfoPolicy` first:** a death's, a hatching's and an egg's crack ask `Emotes`, the trail `Markers`. | #306 | §6.4, §6.9 |
