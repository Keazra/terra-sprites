# Motion

Small effects drawn over the finished screen in real time, with one switch that turns them all off. Fauxpaw settled it in the UI lessons round of 2026-10-19 (question 7), and the six further ideas the same day, as Look 5 ([#306](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/306)) of the Look slices ([#301](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/301)).

## M1 "A Sprite Lives"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Motion is drawn over the finished screen by tachyonfx,** in `terra-tui` only, timed by real time, not ticks. Each frame hands the app the time since the last, and tests and the renderer hand it a fixed step. Drawing stays pure: each frame builds every effect afresh from when it began. | #306 | §6.6, §6.8, §6.9 |
| 2 | **One switch turns all motion off,** on by default; `--no-motion` starts the game with it off. The older blinking, which tells the player something, stays. | #306 | §6.7, §6.9 |
| 3 | **A death flashes its tile and leaves a ghost** of the sprite fading to ash grey, since the sprite has left the world by the time the screen hears it died. **A hatching sparks** round the egg's tile, and the young warms from the egg's colour to its own, the crack playing as it appears, since the screen can't tell in time when an egg will hatch. | #306, read for Fauxpaw on 2026-10-27 | §6.9 |
| 4 | **A tab opened sweeps in, a pop-up fades in and out, water shimmers between two shades, a drive's bar eases a block at a time, the view pans to a sprite, and a fading trail follows the selected sprite.** The call ripple waits for Slice 34's waves (#273). | #306 | §6.9 |
| 5 | **Few things move at once, and motion thins out at 4× and up:** deaths, hatchings and the trail are shorter and fewer, and the bars and the view jump. | #306 | §6.9 |
| 6 | **The pan moves only what's drawn:** the viewport jumps at once, the pointer and clicks follow the drawing, and the Cursor moves only when the pan ends, so the world never hears of the tiles between. | #306 | §6.9 |
| 7 | **A theme's `motion` section gives motion's colours,** exact ones, and a named colour blends from how Windows Terminal's Campbell scheme draws it. A theme without the section uses the default's. | #306, #302 | §6.2, §6.9 |
| 8 | **Motion that tells the player something asks the `InfoPolicy` first:** a death's and a hatching's ask `Emotes`, the trail `Markers`. | #306 | §6.4, §6.9 |
