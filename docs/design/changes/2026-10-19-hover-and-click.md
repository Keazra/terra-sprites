# What the pointer is over, and a click on a log line

Whatever the pointer is over says what it is in a tooltip, and a click on a sprite's name in the event log selects it. Fauxpaw settled it in the UI lessons round of 2026-10-19 (question 4), as Look 3 ([#304](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/304)) of the Look slices ([#301](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/301)). The tabs' clicks came in Look 1 ([#302](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/302)); the rest was built in [#388](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/388). While it was built, on 2026-10-26, Fauxpaw chose a tooltip below and to the left of the pointer in place of the status line, a colour of its own for whatever can be explained, and only a gene's name lit, its numbers kept on its line.
