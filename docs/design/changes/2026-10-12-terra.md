# Terra, the ship (M4), and the view past the wall

The ship's design pass ([#181](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/181), with [#180](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/180) and [#26](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/26)'s zones), settled with the owner in six rounds on 2026-10-12, in [PR #185](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/185). Rounds 1 and 2 designed the view past the wall, M2's Slice 31 ([#184](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/184)); round 4 named Story mode and Lab mode; and the ship became a design of its own, [`m4-terra.md`](../m4-terra.md). The ship is called Terra, and its milestone, M4, comes after Body & verbs, so the later milestones move up one: Wild Terra is M5 and Words M6. The README's roadmap, the glossary and a registry comment follow.
