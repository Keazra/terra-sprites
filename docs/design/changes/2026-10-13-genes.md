# Mixing and mutating genes

Slice 24's first part, 24a ([#142](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/142), [PR #193](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/193)): children differ from their parents, and the quality of their genes joins ageing. Settled with the owner in the slice's design round, answered 2026-10-13.

## M2 "Generations"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Copying errors:** when an egg is laid, each gene value has `mutation.chance` (0.01) of changing; three changes in four (`large.share`) are a factor from half to double (`large.most` 2), the rest within a fifth (`small`). Only values change, held within their ranges, so a flagged gene stays flagged. A sprite remembers each gene copying changed and what it was. A pack without `mutation` copies exactly. | Owner, Q1 (2026-10-13): rare and large, a few small, "fun and visible-ish"; Q2: a rule of the world | §12.1 |
| 2 | **A pair's genes line up by what each gene does,** not where it sits, and mix in stretches: the egg switches parent at each gene with `breeding.crossover`'s chance (0.1). A gene only one parent has goes to about half the young. A pack whose `breeding` has no `crossover` mixes gene by gene (0.5). | Owner, Q3 | §10.5, §12.2 |
| 3 | **Hidden flaws:** each gene that can weaken has two copies, one from each parent; one sound copy is enough. A parent passes one copy at random; a lone egg gets one of its mother's, doubled. Each sound copy gets a flaw in copying with `flaws.arise` (0.007), about one egg in two; a sprite made from the starter carries one in each such gene with `flaws.founders` (0.06), about three, and one from a genome file none. Checked every tick. | Owner, Q5 | §3, §10.3, §12.3 |
| 4 | **A broken gene,** both copies flawed, is born with `flaws.health` (0.5) and counts as `flaws.wear` (0.05) of wear towards growing old sooner and decaying faster. | Owner, Q6 | §9.4, §12.4 |
| 5 | **No energy cost for a long life:** `ageing.upkeep` stays 0, and lives may grow longer over the generations, up to `grows_old`'s ceiling. | Owner, Q7 (2026-10-13): "let Sprites die due to failure of the body/brain as opposed to an artificial energy cost" | §2, §3, §9.3, §10.8, §12.5 |
| 6 | **The Genome tab opens with what's new:** where the genes came from, each gene copying changed and what it was, and the genes broken and carried. No new log lines. | Owner, Q9 (B) | §12.6 |
| 7 | **How we know it worked:** 200,000 ticks, seeds 1–10: at least 3 gene numbers moved a tenth the same way in all 10 (G4), sprites alive in at least 8, never over the limit; A1–A5 still met (A3 on seeds 1–30). No bar on life length. | Owner, Q10, Q7 | §6, §12.7 |
| 8 | **Later:** copy and change as 24b; the error rate as a gene (#194); stress damaging genes (#195). | Owner, Q2, Q4, Q8 | §12.8 |
