# Nests

The owner proposed Nests on 2026-10-27: a family's home on the map, with warmth for its eggs and a Nest Area around it that grows with the family. The design is the owner's. Its round, settled with the owner the same day, placed it in M3 straight after Slice 41 as two slices ([Slice 41b: Nests](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/396), [Slice 41c: Nest Areas](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/397)), settled how sprites aim at a tile, and put eggs beside the Nest rather than on it, all as recommended.
