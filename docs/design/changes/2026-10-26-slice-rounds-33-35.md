# The rounds of Slices 33, 34 and 35

The design rounds of M3's first three slices, each settled with the owner on 2026-10-26: [Slice 33: Limbs that hurt and heal](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/359), [Slice 34: Grief for what a sprite sees](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/360) and [Slice 35: Hands](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/361). Each issue's resolution comment is its record. Slice 34's round also brought calls drawn as waves ([#273](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/273)) into the slice, at the owner's ask.

## M3 "Body & verbs"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A hurt limb heals in about half an hour of play,** an old sprite's more slowly, and rest doesn't speed it. That's B1's number. A hurt lands on the limb the act used, as each thing's data says. A sore limb works three quarters as well, one useless leg walks at half speed and both crawl at a quarter, and a second hurt can make a limb useless for up to a quarter of an hour. A sore head or arm fumbles some tries, by the dice in one place. The brain feels its legs, its better arm and its head. | #359's round | §5.2, §6.4, §7.4 |
| 2 | **Grief is a drive you can see,** a bar and a colour on the map. It holds arousal down, leans away from play, lowers curiosity, draws the sprite to its family and fades faster in company. The young grieve too. A full spell is for a partner, its own young or, while young, a parent, and a light one for other kin, less for kin it knew less well. Giving up brings half the grief. The search goes back to where the missing one was last seen, and the spot stays as a fainter pull. The words on screen are set. | #360's round | §8.4 |
| 3 | **Calls are drawn as waves:** rings for the selected sprite's calls and the calls it hears, rolling round walls as the sound does, with a key for every call and a flourish when a dance is answered. Front end only. Look 5's call ripple (#306) waits for it. | #360's round, the owner's ask; #273's own round, 2026-10-17 | §1.1, §3, §8.5 |
| 4 | **Hands are used from the start:** boredom's pull to pick up and throw starts weak, and a sprite puts things down when nothing else pulls on it or when it's hurt. A throw rolls like the Cursor's, and the sprite lines up first. Drop carries the thing to what the sprite is looking at. A sprite feels how full its arms are and whether what it's looking at is the thing in its arms. The doing line, the log and the status line say what it holds and throws.
