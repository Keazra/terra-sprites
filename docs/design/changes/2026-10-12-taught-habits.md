# What the Cursor teaches lasts, and lessons fade slowly

A3 had passed or failed by luck since Milestone 1: one shock wore off for 9 sprites in 10, and it took three to last ([#187](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/187)). On 2026-10-12 the owner chose to fix it before breeding merged. This is the fix recommended to them, "Lasting lessons": what the Cursor teaches is kept apart and lasts, in [PR #189](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/189). Asked how long the Cursor's lessons should last, the owner answered for every lesson (2026-10-12): "Lessons shouldn't fade quickly, in general, whether self-taught or corrected/rewarded. But I'm fine with lessons taught by the Cursor being more impactful", as lessons taught by other sprites may be later (Slice 28).

**Measured** over A3's seeds 1–30, with `cargo run --release -p terra-sim --example lab -- scenarios/a3-correct-training.ron --seeds 30`: trained sprites' median of hits in ticks 10,000–20,000 was 23 on `main` at `3b246f3` and 0 with taught habits at `acb2809`, against 69 for the control both times. Every random set of 10 of those seeds passes, against 84 in 100 on `main`.

**Measured again with every lesson fading slowly** (row 3), at `a79e4dd`, against the same branch with the old fades, with the `lab` example: A3 over seeds 1–30, trained 0 against 48 for the control (0 against 69 before; the untrained sprites' inborn wariness now lasts longer too), and every random set of 10 seeds passes. A1 on seeds 1–10: 1 thornbush touch for a learning sprite in ticks 0–20,000 against 40 for the control (2 before). A2 on seeds 1–10: 8,464.5 ball plays trained against 209 for the control (5,456 against 233.5 before). A4, `scenarios/viability.ron` on seeds 1–10: every sprite alive at tick 10,000, and 1 of 228 deaths in ticks 10,000–50,000 was of hunger, against 3 of 277 of thirst before.

## M1 "A Sprite Lives"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A Reward or Correct for what a sprite just tried teaches its taught habit,** kept apart from the habit it learns by itself, at `taught_rate`, three times `habit_rate`. It fades by `taught_fade`, as slowly as a bad lesson (row 3). The decision, the Brain tab and the lessons count the two together, within ±1. A pet or shock that finds no try within reach still teaches the sprite's own habits along the trace, as before, and a sprite nobody trains learns exactly as before. Saves from before load with no taught habits; a replay recorded before parts from its recording at its start, as one does between builds that share a version number. | #187 | §5.5, §5.6, §5.8.1, §7.2 |
| 2 | **Two new brain parameters,** `taught_rate` (ID 39, 0–1, default 0.9) and `taught_fade` (ID 40, 0–0.01, default 0.00000625), so how trainable a sprite is can differ between sprites and evolve once genomes carry them. The starter genome doesn't yet, as for most brain parameters: a new gene would change every sprite's spawn variation. A data pack from before them gets the built-in range and default. Appendix A also lists 37 and 38, which came with M2 (M2 design §7.2, §9.5). | #187 | Appendix A, Appendix B |
| 3 | **Every lesson fades slowly:** good worth and good habits by half in about 28,000 ticks (`worth_fade_good`, `habit_fade` 0.000025), about 6 hours at 1×, an average life; bad worth, bad habits, fear and taught habits in about 110,000 (`worth_fade_bad`, `habit_fade_bad`, `fear_fade`, `taught_fade` 0.00000625), so in effect for life. Bad still fades four times more slowly than good. They halved in about 3,500 and 14,000 ticks before. A remembered place still halves in about 3,500 ticks: it can change under the sprite. | Owner (2026-10-12) | §5.6, Appendix B; M2 §7.2 |
