# Courtship and families

Slice 32 ([#186](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/186)): sprites call for a mate, court before mating, stay together as partners, and keep near their eggs and young, and lone eggs become very rare. Settled with the owner in two rounds of questions on 2026-10-13.

## M2 "Generations"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Calls:** a new action, `Call`, with no target, costing `calls.cost` energy (0.002). A call travels like a wave over every tile whose terrain carries sound, round the rest and through doorways, as far as its way costs at most `calls.reach` (300). Each terrain's `sound` in `terrain.ron` says how much it soaks up per tile (10 for every open terrain in M2), and one with none stops it. A hearer feels a `heard` pulse, and for `calls.heard_for` ticks (150) the caller is offered at the edge of sight, as a remembered place is. Going to it is a trip. | Owner, round 1 Q3; round 2 Q3, "It travels like a wave" | §15.1 |
| 2 | **Hearing counts for sex by neighbours,** and for breeding alone: an adult heard calling within `heard_for` ticks counts as near for `female_near` and `male_near`. | Owner, round 1 Q4 | §10.1, §15.1 |
| 3 | **Familiarity with an individual:** how well a sprite knows each sprite it remembers, from 0 to 1. It grows by `befriend_rate` (0.0013) a tick in each other's sight and by `befriend_play` (0.1) for each social Play between them, and fades by `befriend_fade` (0.000025), all brain parameters. Seeing a sprite is enough to remember it. Body language reads it, `target_familiar`. | Owner, round 1 Q1: "only after they know one another" | §15.2 |
| 4 | **Courtship:** a new action, `Court`, which walks up to the sprite attended to and dances once, offered only between adults. The one danced for feels `courted`, the dancer `danced`. A new hormone, **receptivity**, which the starter genes raise on both, and Mate lays an egg only when the female's is at least `breeding.receptive` (0.5). In the starter genes he dances, she chooses, and she dances back. | Owner, round 1 Q1 and Q2 ("I like the hormonal angle more"); round 2 Q1 | §5, §10.3, §15.3 |
| 5 | **She chooses by how healthy he looks:** body language reads `target_health`, the sprite's energy, hydration and freedom from injury, times its genes' average health. | Owner, round 2 Q2 | §15.3 |
| 6 | **Partners:** two sprites that have an egg together are partners, for life in the starter genes. A partner is freed when the other dies (a `lost_partner` pulse) or after `breeding.apart` (4,500) ticks without seeing or hearing each other. Mourning is a starter hormone, `grief`, that holds arousal down for about 1,500 ticks. New body sensor `partner_near` and body language `target_partner`. | Owner, round 1 Q5 | §15.4 |
| 7 | **Kin:** a sprite knows its parents, its young, and its brothers and sisters, and body language reads `target_kin`. The starter genes weigh strongly against courting kin; nothing forbids it. | Owner, round 1 Q6: "strong pressure against mating with family, but not impossible" | §15.5 |
| 8 | **Families and anxiety (32b):** both parents keep near their egg and their young until the young grow up; one can leave to eat while the other stays. A new drive and second-order need, **anxiety**, with body sensors `egg_away`, `egg_alone`, `young_away`, `parent_away` and `partner_away`. An attention instinct can name whom it's about, its family or its kin. A parent remembers where it last saw its egg and goes back to it on a trip. The egg category offers a parent its own egg first. | Owner, round 1 Q7 and Q8; round 2 Q4 | §5, §15.6 |
| 9 | **Breeding alone is the body's rule:** a female can lay alone only after `breeding.alone_after` (4,500) ticks with no male adult seen or heard, which replaces the gate on longing, `breeds_alone`.
