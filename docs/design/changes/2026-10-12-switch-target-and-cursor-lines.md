# A change of mind keeps its target; the Cursor's own lines ask the policy

Two small questions left open by the M1 acceptance check (#126), settled with the owner on 2026-10-12: [#173](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/173) and [#174](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/174), in [PR #182](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/pull/182).

## M1 "A Sprite Lives"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A verb switched to mid-action is aimed at the running action's target,** the thing the decision weighed, not at the candidate attention now picks in its category. Which verbs are offered goes by that target too: no Approach once beside it, no Retreat from a remembered place out of sight, and only Rest and Wander while it's out of reach (a retreat's target can be), since there's no way to it. Before, a sprite walking to one bush that decided to eat could eat a nearer one, a thornbush included. | #173 | §5.3, §5.5 |
| 2 | **A new panel, `CursorLines`,** the Cursor's own lines: the status line's parts about the sprite it follows and what it leads or holds, the prompt for naming a sprite, and a refused click's reason should one ever name a sprite (none does yet). Each asks about the sprite it names, or the world for an item held; denied, the part is left out, and the prompt reads "Name: Pip_". M1's policy shows everything, so nothing looks different; a future Play mode decides once, there, whether these lines show. | #174 | §6.4 |
