# Room nearby

Room nearby was settled with the owner on 2026-10-20 in [#318](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/318), as Slice 29b ([#331](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/331)): a grown sprite can breed only where fewer than 4 other grown sprites are within 10 tiles, so that roaming pays again in a world that sits full. On 2026-10-24 the owner held Slice 29's missed long-run bars over to [#332](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/332), reported but not must-pass. What the screen says was settled in Slice 29b's round.
