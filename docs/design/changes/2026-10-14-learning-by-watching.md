# Learning by watching

Slice 28 ([#146](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/146), from [#66](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/66)): a sprite takes on a share of what the sprite its eye is on knows about what it uses, and of what happens to it; everyone does, the young most, watching the grown-ups, their parents most, and a parent showing its young something it hasn't learned draws its eye. Settled with the owner in two rounds of questions on 2026-10-14: nine in round 1, and the bar in round 2 once `main` was measured on the new arena. The owner took the recommendations but for Q1, going further, and Q7, everyone.
