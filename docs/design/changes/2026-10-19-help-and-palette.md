# Help for the screen you're on, and a command palette

The key hints, the help and a new command palette are built from one list of commands. Fauxpaw settled it in the UI lessons round of 2026-10-19 (question 3, answer A), as Look 2 ([#303](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/303)) of the Look slices ([#301](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/301)).

## M1 "A Sprite Lives"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One list of commands makes the key hints, the help and the command palette,** so they can't drift apart: each command's keys, what it does, its help headings, its hint, and what it needs to do anything. | #303 | §6.1 |
| 2 | **A hint whose key would do nothing just now is greyed** rather than hidden, its key muted like its word: `. step` while time runs, `↑↓ choose` with nothing to choose, and in a replay the keys it refuses, such as `X train`. | #303 | §6.1 |
| 3 | **The help screen has five pages,** named in its top edge: World, every key and the legends as before, with `:` and the files' keys added; Inspector, its keys, the pointer's and the open tab's legend; and a page each for the sprite list, the population graph and the family tree. A page too long to fit scrolls. | #303 | §6.1 |
| 4 | **`?` opens help at the page for where the player is:** the open overlay's, the inspector's once the player last pointed at it or used its keys, otherwise the world's. `←` `→` and `Tab` `Shift+Tab` turn the pages, and closing it goes back to what was open. | #303 | §6.1 |
| 5 | **Typing in help searches every page,** each match under its page's name; `Esc` clears the search before it closes the help. Keys type rather than act while help is open, where before they did nothing. | #303 | §6.1, §6.5 |
| 6 | **`:` opens a command palette** near the top of the map view: typing finds any key that works in the world by the starts of the words of what it does, each with its key, and `Enter` does the one chosen. | #303 | §6.1, §6.5 |
| 7 | **The hints and help write keys in lower case,** `esc` and `tab`, as the hints did, and `+/-` for the speed keys. | #303 | §6.1 |
