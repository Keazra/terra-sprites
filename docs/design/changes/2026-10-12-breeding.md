# Breeding

Slice 23 ([#141](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/141)): grown sprites have young, only ever between adults. Settled with the owner in the slice's design round, from 2026-10-12.

## M2 "Generations"

| # | Change | Source | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Old age lasts about an hour,** so a sprite lives about 4 hours: `decay.ticks` is 3,600, down from 46,500, so a sprite with an easy life is old for about 4,000 ticks. When decay needs more than one hit a tick, as for a worn sprite, the whole hits land and the odds are of one more. | M2 planning (#141) | §2, §9.4 |
| 2 | **Mate is offered only when the sprite and its target are both adults** (old counts), checked when it chooses, and the self-check fails on any Mate action that isn't between adults. Property tests with random genomes pin it. A save from before Mate gets its column in the brain at 0. | #69, M2 planning | §5 |
| 3 | **A lab scenario can have its sprites grow old at another age** (`grows_old`), and A1 to A3 and the thorn trap use 60,000 ticks, as before M2: they watch the same sprites for 20,000 to 30,000 ticks, longer than a 4-hour life. | Owner, Q10 (2026-10-12) | §2 |
| 4 | **Sexes, by neighbours:** every sprite hatches male; an adult male turns female for good once its `feminiser` hormone is above `turns_female` (0.8). New loci `female`, `female_near` (body sensors) and `bred` (a pulse); the starter genes raise the feminiser while no female adult is in sight and lower it fast while one is. Only a female lays eggs. | Owner, Q3 card (2026-10-12) | §10.1 |
| 5 | **Fertility and arousal:** fertile while grown, energy and hydration above `fed` (0.5), `rest` (1,500) ticks after its last egg, in a world with room. Arousal, a new drive and need (first called broodiness), rises with fertility and drops at once with breeding or within a few ticks of fertility's end. Its relief teaches worth only after a Mate. | Owner, Q3 (2026-10-12); `fed` and the name, 2026-10-12 | §10.2 |
| 6 | **Mate and BreedAlone:** two verbs chosen by instinct. Mate lays an egg between a fertile male and female, who split its `cost` (0.3); BreedAlone lays one for a fertile female alone, who pays it all. BreedAlone is offered only to a grown sprite. | Owner, Q1, Q2, Q4 | §5, §10.3 |
| 7 | **Body language:** brain inputs can read the sprite attended to, `Target(...)`, or how far it differs from the sprite's own, `Differs(...)`; the built-in pack has `target_arousal` (71) and `target_other_sex` (72), and the starter mates only with an aroused sprite of the other sex. | Owner, card (2026-10-12) | §10.3 |
| 8 | **Eggs:** a new first life stage. An egg is laid beside its mother, feels and needs nothing, and hatches as a young male after `hatches` (750) ticks. Sprites see it as the category `egg` (8, so `attended_egg` is input 45) and the pseudo type `egg` (103), with no verbs; the Cursor can't touch, lead or hold it. A sprite remembers its parents. | Owner, Q5 | §9.1, §10.4 |
| 9 | **A child's genes are mixed,** gene by gene with its switch, from its parents where their genomes line up, or else its mother's. | Owner, Q6 | §10.5 |
| 10 | **The limit:** a preset's `most_sprites` (100 by default, 1 to 1,000, at least its sprites), eggs counted. A full world has no fertile sprite. A lab scenario can set it, and A1 to A3 and the thorn trap hold no more than they start with. | Owner, Q7 | §10.6 |
| 11 | **What the player sees:** log lines for laying, hatching and turning female; an egg drawn `o` (`0` in ASCII); the Body tab's sex, fertility, turning and parents; arousal's colour. | Owner, Q8 | §10.7 |
| 12 | **How we know it worked:** 100,000 ticks, seeds 1–10, sprites alive in at least 8, never over the limit; A1–A5 still met. | Owner, Q9 | §10.8 |
| 13 | **The breeding drive works only between adults,** for every genome: `physiology.ron` names it (`breeding.drive`), and it reads 0 in a sprite not grown up and in an adult's choices about one, its attention, its decision and the body language it reads. Its fall teaches only about the partner it mated with, never sprites in general or new things. | #69; the slice's review | §5, §10.2 |
| 14 | **A female breeds alone only once her longing has reached `breeds_alone` (0.5):** a new hormone, `longing` (49, brain input 73), which the starter genes raise while she sees no male adult and lower fast while she sees one, and a new body sensor, `male_near`. Before it, 9 eggs in 10 were laid alone, most from chance tries. A stopgap until Slice 32 (#186) brings courtship and families, after which lone eggs should be very rare. | Owner (2026-10-12): a social species, lone eggs very rare | §10, §10.1, §10.3 |

The reference docs moved some example IDs that the new data took: the mod example chemical `chill` is 26 and its brain input 74, the example locus `splashed` 44, and the example category `tree` 9.
