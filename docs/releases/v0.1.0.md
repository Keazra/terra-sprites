# v0.1.0: A Sprite Lives

The first milestone: a terrarium of sprites that eat, drink, play, get hurt and learn from all of it, and a Cursor you can reach in with. Sprites live and die, but they don't breed or grow up yet; that's v0.2.0.

## What's new

- **A terrarium from a seed.** Berry bushes grow from seedlings, fruit, spread and die. Thornbushes spread, water sits in pools, and balls roll, bounce, knock other balls on and squash berries. The same seed gives the same world every time, on Windows, Mac or Linux.
- **Sprites that run on instinct at first.** A hungry one goes to a bush and eats, a thirsty one drinks, a bored one kicks a ball, a lonely one goes over to the others, and a tired one rests. One that gets hit usually backs off, sometimes hits back, and fights if it's cornered.
- **Sprites that learn by doing.** Bite a thornbush and get pricked, and the log says it learned "thornbushes are bad". Sprites learn what's good when they're hungry or thirsty, which habits don't work, and who to steer clear of, without turning shy of everyone. Lessons fade unless they're renewed, and instinct is never wiped out, only outvoted.
- **The Cursor.** In Train mode a click pets a sprite and a right click zaps it, and it learns from both. In Grab mode you pick things up, lead a sprite somewhere, throw things and shove sprites. The Place menu adds berries, balls, bushes and new sprites. `F` makes the Cursor follow a sprite, and `H` lets the sprites see it, so they learn to like or fear it from how you treat them.
- **A look inside.** Select a sprite to see its body, its Brain tab (what it's paying attention to, why, and everything it remembers), its chemistry and its genes. You can name it, and save its genes to place a copy later.
- **Time, saves and replays.** Pause, step, and run from ⅛× up to 16× and Max. Worlds save and load, and save themselves every 10 minutes. Every session is kept as a replay you can watch again, or take over partway with `Ctrl+R`.
- **Your own world.** The ground, the plants and toys, a sprite's body and instincts, and how the map is drawn are all data files you can change, with `--data`, `--preset` and themes.

## Known problems

- In terminals that use the kitty keyboard protocol (not Windows Terminal), `?` doesn't open the help, and names you type lose their capitals ([#124](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/124), fixed in v0.2.0).
- Resting never counts as relief from tiredness, so sprites can't learn that it helps ([#125](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/125)).
- This version opens straight onto a terrarium; the title screen came in v0.2.0.

## Install

You need [Rust](https://rustup.rs) 1.97.1 or newer (1.97.0 builds the simulation wrong), and a terminal of at least 100×30; on Windows, Windows Terminal.

```bash
cargo install --git https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites --tag v0.1.0 --locked terra-tui
terra-sprites --seed 7
```
