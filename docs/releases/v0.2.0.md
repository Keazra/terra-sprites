# v0.2.0: Generations

The second milestone. Sprites now hatch, grow up, pair off, raise their young and grow old, and each child gets a mix of its parents' genes, now and then with something nobody asked for. Water is scarce, so a newborn on its own rarely makes it unless its family, or you, step in. Leave a world running and the species changes, not always for the better.

## What's new

- **A title screen.** A light finds a sleeping sprite in the dark, and a terrarium wakes up around it, already going about its day without you. Any key skips the scene. In a world, `Esc` now offers the title screen as well as quitting, and both save first.
- **Life stages.** Sprites hatch young: small (`ö`), slow, curious and quick to learn. They grow up after about an hour at 1×, grow old about five hours in, and die about an hour after that. A hard life wears a sprite out sooner, as hard lives do.
- **Eggs.** Every sprite hatches male, and a grown male who sees no female for a while turns female for good. It's a practical arrangement. A fed, watered and rested pair lays an egg (`o`), which hatches about ten minutes later. You can carry eggs in Grab mode, but not throw them. Only grown-ups ever court or mate, whatever their genes or your data files say.
- **Courtship and families.** Sprites call for a mate (`♪`), heard up to about 30 tiles away, dance (`♫`), and pair up, usually for life. Partners stay near their egg, and near their young until those grow up, and an anxious parent, tinted light green, goes back for one it can't see. Sprites shy away from courting their kin.
- **Room to breed.** A world holds up to 100 sprites and eggs, and a sprite with four or more grown-ups near it holds off breeding, so sprites keep roaming rather than settling into a crowd.
- **A harsher world.** Water is scarce, in a few big lakes. Sprites act only when something moves them, so many stand and watch, and the young wander little: a newborn far from water rarely grows up on its own. Food and drink share one gut, and berry bushes now seed. The old, watery world is still there: put [`gentle.ron`](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/raw/v0.2.0/data/presets/gentle.ron) in a `presets` folder in the game's folder (`?` shows where that is), and New world offers it.
- **Genes that change.** Copying isn't perfect: most hatchlings have a number or two that came out different, and about one in ten a whole gene doubled, lost or changed. Hidden flaws only bite when a sprite gets the same one from both parents. The Genome tab opens with the news, and the log mentions anything new.
- **Learning by watching.** The young learn from whoever they watch, their parents most: a youngster that sees its mother drink learns that water helps thirst, and where the lake is. Your pets and zaps rub off on onlookers too, so mind what you do in front of the children.
- **Remembered places.** A thirsty sprite with no water in sight walks back to water it remembers. If what it remembers is gone, like a bush that died, it forgets the place and doesn't dwell on it.
- **The family tree** (`k`) shows grandparents, parents, partner, young and siblings, living or dead, and `b`'s lineage mode lights up a sprite's relatives on the map.
- **The population graph** (`p`) shows the whole run, split into eggs, young, adults and old, with each moment's generations underneath.
- **Story and Lab worlds.** Story is the game as it's meant to be played, up to 4×. Lab is for experiments, up to Max. A world keeps its mode for good, so choose with some care.
- **Fast-forward.** `terra-sprites --fast-forward 200 --seed 7` runs 200 hours of play with no screen, then saves the world and writes tables for a spreadsheet. About ninety generations pass in the time of a long dinner.
- **The view past the wall.** The map scrolls out past the terrarium's glass, into space and drifting stars. The sprites haven't noticed any of this.
- **Smaller things.** `/` filters the sprite list, `F3` shows how well the game is keeping up, `--version` says what you're running, a renderer makes clips of the game, and the game is now yours to share under MIT or Apache 2.0.
- **Downloads.** No Rust needed: each release has a ready-made game for Windows, Mac (Apple silicon) and Linux.

## Improved

- **Lessons last.** Good lessons fade over about a lifetime; bad ones, and anything you teach with the Cursor, last for life. They remember now.
- **A tidier inspector.** The Brain tab groups lessons under what they're about, a selected sprite shows in reverse video at any age, the mouse wheel works in menus, and the top bar counts the seconds since the world saved, rather than saying "just now" for a whole minute.
- **A manual.** The README is a short front page, and everything else lives in the [manual](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/blob/v0.2.0/docs/manual.md).

## Fixed

- `?` and capital letters work in kitty-protocol terminals: kitty, WezTerm, foot and Ghostty ([#124](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/124)).
- A sprite could stand frozen for most of a minute when its target moved out of reach. It now gets on with its life.
- A sprite walking to one bush could eat a nearer one instead, even a thornbush.
- While paused, a loaded world's names and your grabs show at once, and letting go of `?` always registers.
- Damaged saves and replays are refused with a message saying what's wrong, rather than crashing the game.
- Installing with Rust 1.97.0, which builds the game wrong, now stops and asks you to update.

## Before you upgrade

- **Story worlds stop at 4×.** Start a Lab world (`--lab`) to go faster. Worlds from v0.1.0 load as Story worlds.
- **Worlds from v0.1.0 never breed.** They load and carry on, and their sprites now grow up and grow old, but never have young. Start a new world to see sprites breed.
- **Replays from v0.1.0** play only in v0.1.0.
- **On kitty-protocol terminals,** `>`, `_`, `{` and `}` no longer stand in for `.`, `-`, `[` and `]`.
- **A `--data` folder from v0.1.0** with a changed `chemicals.ron`, `loci.ron`, `brain_io.ron` or `categories.ron` won't load: start again from v0.2.0's copy of that file. Themes still work.

## Known problems

- Grown sprites die of hunger or thirst, and try to eat while too full, a little more often than planned, and partners keep each other in sight a little under half the time ([#332](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/332)).
- Over many generations, sprites evolve to live more than twice as long, but not to do better: in a fresh world, the evolved ones raise fewer young than the first sprites' genes do. Nobody promised evolution would be progress ([#332](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/332)).
- Resting never counts as relief from tiredness, so sprites can't learn that it helps ([#125](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/125)).
- The downloads aren't signed, so Windows or a Mac may balk the first time (the zip's `README.txt` says what to do), and Windows with Smart App Control on won't run it at all: install it with Rust there instead ([#352](https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites/issues/352)).

## Install

Download the zip for your computer from Assets, below: Windows, Mac (Apple silicon) or Linux. Its `README.txt` says how to run it. Or, with Rust 1.97.1 or newer:

    cargo install --git https://github.com/Keazra/terra-sprites --tag v0.2.0 --locked terra-tui

Either way, you need a terminal of at least 100×30 (on Windows, Windows Terminal).
